Fixing Vertec With AI

Can we do better?

Henry Russell

Timing: 2 minutes. Open with the recognisable groan, then refuse the cheap shot. The joke is not "people hate admin". The joke is that a serious organisation pays clever, expensive people to act as copy, paste, long-term memory, policy engine, and QA team for a grid of tiny fields, every Friday, until retirement or death, whichever the calendar widget permits first. Say the running example is Vertec, but the real subject is redesigning awkward enterprise workflows with AI without switching our brains off.

Cybernetic Delivery

Not tool rollout. A feedback system.

The CDM lens is simple enough for this room: make artefact flow visible, shorten feedback loops, measure the system, keep humans accountable for judgement.

Timing: 2 minutes. Anchor this in the Camp template language: Cybernetic Delivery is not a Scrum replacement, sales play, AI campaign, or "buy a tool and hope morale improves" plan. It is a way to improve how the delivery system learns and adapts. For this workshop, Vertec is the small system we can all recognise without needing a three-month discovery phase.

Timing: 2 minutes. Make the trade-off explicit. Direct APIs are often the right technical answer, but they remove the accidental friction of the UI. That means we must add intentional friction: dry runs, diffs, source citations, confirmations, logs, and permission scopes. The superapp question

Is Codex signed into everything the future?

Maybe. The useful version is a scoped workbench: tools, permissions, traces, and confirmations. The silly version is "please operate my life" and a shrug.

Timing: 2 minutes. Use the current Codex direction carefully: plugins, Sites, annotations, and tool workflows point toward more work happening inside one agentic workspace. But the workshop stance is not "let it do everything". It is "make the boundary explicit enough that a sceptical teammate can inspect it".

Takeaway

Find the hidden integration work.

Then decide the smallest thing that makes it visible, testable, and safe enough for a sceptical colleague to run after lunch.

Timing: 1 minute. Close on consultancy craft. The Vertec joke gets people in the room, but the transferable skill is spotting where humans are bridging systems, policy, and memory. AI helps when it sharpens that picture and gives us inspectable tools. It hurts when we use it to bury the mess under a faster clicker and a confident tone.
