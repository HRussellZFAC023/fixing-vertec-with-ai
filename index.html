Fixing Vertec With AI

Can we do better?

Henry Russell

Timing: 2 minutes. Open with the recognisable groan, then refuse the cheap shot. The joke is not "people hate admin". The joke is that a serious organisation pays clever, expensive people to act as copy, paste, long-term memory, policy engine, and QA team for a grid of tiny fields, every Friday, until retirement or death, whichever the calendar widget permits first. Say the running example is Vertec, but the real subject is redesigning awkward enterprise workflows with AI without switching our brains off.

Cybernetic Delivery

Not tool rollout. A feedback system.

The CDM lens is simple enough for this room: make artefact flow visible, shorten feedback loops, measure the system, keep humans accountable for judgement.

It is a way to improve how the delivery system learns and adapts. For this workshop, Vertec is the small system we can all recognise without needing a three-month discovery phase.

AI literacy

Learn to smell the slop.

The tells: "it's not X, it's Y", em dashes everywhere, ASCII arrows, rule-of-three lists, cards and gradient fog (Codex's signature look), image defects, and, at Zühlke, an umlaut someone clearly typed by hand. Output is raw material. Make it yours before it makes you look haunted.

Timing: 2 minutes. This is where you teach the meta skill. AI text has tells: generic contrast phrases, over-explaining, em dashes everywhere, cards and gradients in UI, weird image defects, confident paragraphs that nobody asked for. Link it back to CDM: feedback loops only work if humans inspect the artefacts and improve them.
